The hip principle

Why hips drive everything else.

Tight hip flexors pull on the lumbar spine. Weak glutes push load onto the right knee. Every session opens with hip prep — it's not extra, it's the foundation that makes everything else work.

Operating principles

  • Never skip hip prep. It's not a warmup — it's the mechanism that keeps your back and right knee quiet. 5 minutes. Every session. No exceptions.
  • Protein is still the bottleneck. Aim for 90–100g/day over this program. Add a scoop to coffee, extra beans at dinner. You can't out-train low protein.
  • Sharp joint pain = stop. Muscle burn is the program working. Joint pain is data. Drop weight, narrow ROM, or swap the exercise.
  • Sleep is the program too. Muscle grows during recovery. Prioritize it especially in weeks 7 and 11.
  • Reassess at week 12. Use your week 11 data to set week 1 loads for cycle 2 — start at what you finished, not where you began.
